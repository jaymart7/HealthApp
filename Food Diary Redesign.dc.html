Turn 1

Food diary — one refined direction

Three pinned blocks become two. The date row absorbs the filter as a 48dp icon button, so the field only exists while it is being used. The summary stays pinned but has two sizes: full at the top of the day, and a slim always-visible bar (remaining figure + 4dp macro bar) once the list scrolls. The three logging flows leave the text field and become a labelled chip row at the start of the scroll — icon plus word, outlined rather than filled, so they read as entry points and never compete with the filled FAB.

Each meal becomes a surfaceContainerLow card, so five sections read as five objects instead of five grey rules, and the rows inside gain a shared edge to scan down. Exercise leaves the meal run entirely: a small "Adds to today" label with a rule sits above it, its container is outlined on surface rather than filled, its header carries a run icon, and its subtotal is signed — +645 kcal. The same number is restated in the summary as "645 kcal from exercise added to today", which is where the budget arithmetic actually happens, and the consumed line counts against goal + burned.

Screens are live: expand and collapse the sections, tap the filter icon and type, tap water glasses, and drag a food row left past ~96dp to delete it with an Undo snackbar. What moved, and why

Filter → icon. It filters the day's logged entries, which is a thing you do occasionally, on a long day. It cost 64dp permanently. As a 48dp icon in the date row it costs nothing and expands in place; typing keeps the header height identical.

Summary → two sizes. The full block (remaining, consumed/budget, macro bar, legend, micros, exercise credit) is what you see when you open the tab. Past 24dp of scroll it becomes a 44dp bar carrying the two things that matter mid-scroll: the remaining figure and macro fill. Nothing is hidden without a replacement.

Quick actions → labelled chips. Three bare icons next to a field read as field affordances. As outlined chips with words under the summary they read as three ways in. They scroll away; the FAB sheet carries the same three, so they stay one tap from anywhere in the diary.

Meals → cards. 12dp gaps, 16dp radius, surfaceContainerLow. Rows indent to 40dp to clear the chevron, and the card edge gives the calorie column a rail to line up against.

Exercise → separate block. Outlined instead of filled, preceded by an "Adds to today" label and rule, run icon in the header, signed subtotal. Three signals, no new colour.

Over budget is not an error. Same type, same colours; only the word changes, "left" → "over", and the macro fills cap at 100%. No error role, no red, no message.

Dark notes

Meal cards are surfaceContainerLow (#181D18) against surface (#101510) — a 1-step lift, which is enough in dark and needs no border.

The exercise block's outline is outlineVariant, which in dark (#424940) is lighter than the meal cards' fill, so the inversion of the light-mode relationship still reads as "different kind of thing".

Macro bar tracks are surfaceContainerHigh in both modes, so the fill/track contrast never depends on the surface behind the summary.

The mascot is the existing MascotAvatar in its Sleepy state at 48dp — the state Home already uses for an empty diary. Unchanged.

Empty water glasses use outlineVariant; filled use primary with the icon's FILL axis at 1, so the state is shape as well as colour.

Micro-interactions

Expand / collapse
Chevron rotates 0° ⇄ −90° over 200ms, emphasized-decelerate. The card's content height animates over the same 200ms; rows do not fade individually. Collapsing does not scroll — the list settles under the finger. State per section survives day changes within a session.
Summary collapse
Crossfade + height, 200ms, driven by scroll offset crossing 24dp with an 8dp hysteresis band so a jittery finger does not flicker it. Expands again only at scrollTop 0, not on scroll-up, so it never pushes the list mid-read.
Logging a row
New row fades and slides in 12dp over 200ms; the section subtotal and the remaining figure count up over 320ms; macro fills animate width over the same 320ms, emphasized. The remaining figure animates the digits, not a colour flash — including when it crosses into "over".
Swipe to delete
Row translates with the finger, revealing an errorContainer field with a delete icon in onErrorContainer — the one place error appears in the diary, because deletion is destructive. Past 96dp on release the row collapses out and the snackbar appears; short of it, the row springs back over 220ms. This is the only use of the error role on the screen.
Undo
Snackbar 4s at 184dp from the bottom. Undo restores the row in place with the reverse of its entry animation, and the summary counts back down.
Filter
Icon → field is a 150ms crossfade inside the same 48dp row; the field takes focus and the keyboard opens. Rows that fall out of the filter fade over 120ms without reflowing the card's subtotal.

Accessibility

Section header reads "Breakfast, 325 kilocalories, expanded, button". Exercise reads "Exercise, 645 kilocalories burned, added to today's budget".
Forward chevron is enabled = false, not hidden, so its presence and unavailability are both announced.
Water glasses are eight separate toggle buttons ("Water glass 4, filled"), not one composite.
Swipe-to-delete is mirrored as a long-press context action, since a swipe is not reachable by switch access or TalkBack gestures.
At 200% font scale the summary's remaining figure and consumed line stack instead of sitting on one row; legend dots wrap to two lines of two; chip labels drop to icon + tooltip only past 175%.
